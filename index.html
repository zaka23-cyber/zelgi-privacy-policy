Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ZELGI (“we”, “us”, or “our”) collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use the ZELGI iOS app.

ZELGI is an appearance and grooming app. It uses the information you provide, including photos you capture or upload, to build a personal appearance profile, recommendations, a plan, progress comparisons, and optional Generate Look previews.

1. Information we collect

Depending on how you use ZELGI, we may collect:

  • Account information. Email address (if you create an account with email, or if Apple or Google provide it when you sign in) and a unique user/account ID.
  • Photos. Photos you take with the camera or choose from your library for Day 1 analysis, later check-ins, progress / transformation comparisons, and Generate Look. These photos may contain your face and visible appearance.
  • Onboarding and in-app answers. Information you choose to provide, such as age range (not a date of birth), how you want recommendations personalized, goals, hair and grooming details, style preferences, routine frequency, and time budget.
  • App results you create. Appearance scores, analysis notes, plans, check-in comparisons, and saved looks, including generated look images when you save them.
  • Subscription status. Whether a subscription or premium entitlement is active, managed through Apple and RevenueCat. We do not collect your full payment card number. Apple processes the payment.
  • Device-local drafts. Before an account is created, onboarding answers and photos may be stored on your device so you can continue if you leave the app.

ZELGI does not collect location, contacts, health records, advertising identifiers (such as IDFA), or browse other apps. We do not use this information for third-party advertising or cross-app tracking.

2. How we use information

  • Create and authenticate your account.
  • Analyze photos you submit and produce appearance insights.
  • Personalize recommendations, routines, and your plan.
  • Generate Look previews from a photo you provide.
  • Show progress and transformation comparisons across check-in photos.
  • Determine whether premium features are available.
  • Operate, secure, and troubleshoot the service.

3. Face Data and AI Processing

This section describes how ZELGI handles photos that may include your face, and how related appearance data is processed.

What ZELGI collects and processes

ZELGI may process photos containing your face and visible appearance. From a photo and related information you provide, ZELGI may derive:

  • whether a usable single face/person is visible
  • hair length
  • hair styling
  • facial hair
  • facial-hair grooming/tidiness
  • eyebrow grooming
  • visible skin texture appearance
  • visible redness
  • visible shine
  • lighting
  • framing
  • pose
  • distance
  • blur
  • visible clothing
  • limited photo observations
  • appearance-related observations
  • strengths
  • opportunities
  • recommendations
  • confidence values
  • category scores
  • overall appearance score
  • comparison information for check-ins
  • generated look images when requested

What ZELGI does not do

ZELGI does not use these photos to create or store Face ID data, facial recognition identity profiles, facial embeddings, biometric templates, identity verification records, or unique biometric face identifiers. ZELGI does not match your face against a database of other people.

This description is about how the current ZELGI implementation works. It is not a legal classification of the data in every jurisdiction.

Purpose

Photo and appearance data is used to provide the ZELGI features you request:

  • requested appearance analysis
  • personalized appearance insights
  • recommendations
  • progress and check-in comparisons
  • Generate Look previews

OpenAI

ZELGI uses OpenAI, a third-party AI service, for image understanding and image generation. Before ZELGI sends relevant photo or personal appearance data to OpenAI, the app presents a disclosure that names OpenAI and requires you to select “Agree & Continue.” Canceling that disclosure does not send the data.

Feature by feature:

  • Analysis. Your photo is sent to OpenAI together with relevant feature context necessary to generate the requested analysis.
  • Check-in. Your check-in photo and relevant previous appearance information may be sent to OpenAI to produce the requested comparison and analysis.
  • Generate Look. The selected source photo and relevant appearance and style information are sent to OpenAI to create the requested look preview and related output.

Requests go through ZELGI’s backend. OpenAI’s handling of API data is governed by OpenAI’s terms and policies. This policy does not make claims about OpenAI training, OpenAI retention duration, deletion from OpenAI systems, or OpenAI storage behavior.

ZELGI storage

Depending on how you use the app, photos and related analysis data may be stored:

  • on your device, so you can continue a session and see your profile
  • in your private ZELGI cloud photo storage, associated with your account
  • in the ZELGI database as appearance attributes, insights, scores, plans, check-in records, and saved looks

ZELGI’s backend processes analysis and Generate Look requests in order to return results to the app. Photos are not written by the analysis request into long-term backend hosting storage. Private account storage is used later so you can access your profile and track your progress.

Photo storage is private to your account and is not a public gallery. Access to stored photos is restricted to your signed-in account. We do not use your photos for advertising or cross-app tracking.

Retention

ZELGI retains account-associated photos, appearance analysis data, and related records while your ZELGI account exists, unless you delete them through available functionality. There is currently no automated time limit after which ZELGI deletes this data on its own.

Deletion

In the app, go to Settings → Delete my data. That action removes your ZELGI account data, related database records, private ZELGI photo storage, and locally stored ZELGI user files according to the current implementation. This cannot be undone in the app.

This deletion covers ZELGI’s account, database, private photo storage, and local app files. This policy does not promise deletion from third-party provider systems such as OpenAI.

Consent

Before ZELGI sends relevant photo or personal appearance data to OpenAI, the app presents a disclosure identifying OpenAI as a third-party AI service and requires you to explicitly agree. Selecting “Agree & Continue” is that permission. Canceling does not send the photo. Accepting ZELGI’s Terms or Privacy Policy, choosing a photo, or completing onboarding is not treated as this permission.

4. Service providers

Supabase

Authentication, your account, database records (profile, analysis, plan, looks), and private photo storage. Supabase may process your user ID, email address, photos, and the in-app content described above.

Apple and Google

You can sign in with email, Sign in with Apple, or Google. Those providers process the information needed to authenticate you. Apple also processes App Store purchases and subscriptions.

RevenueCat

Subscriptions, purchase restoration, entitlement management, and purchase/subscription status. RevenueCat uses this to tell ZELGI whether premium access is active.

Vercel

Hosts the ZELGI backend used for analysis and Generate Look. Photos and related request data are processed there to return results to the app. Analysis requests are not written into long-term Vercel storage as part of the current implementation.

OpenAI

Used for AI appearance analysis and Generate Look image generation, as described in Face Data and AI Processing.

5. Account creation

You can use parts of onboarding before creating an account. Analysis may run under an anonymous signed-in session so the backend can process your request. When you create an account, your data is associated with that account.

6. Retention and deletion

We keep account data, photos, and related records while your account exists and as needed to provide ZELGI, meet legal obligations, or resolve issues. There is currently no automated deletion schedule.

You can delete your account and the data we store for it in the app: Settings → Delete my data. That action is permanent in the app. It deletes the photos stored in your account’s private storage, your account, and the related ZELGI records we hold (such as profile, analysis, plan, and saved looks). Copies stored on your device are also removed by the app.

We cannot guarantee that Apple, RevenueCat, OpenAI, or other providers immediately or permanently erase every copy they may retain under their own policies (for example, purchase records or short-lived processing copies).

7. Security

We use access controls so that account data and photos are available only to the signed-in user. Photo storage is a private bucket, not a public website. The app talks to our backend over HTTPS. No security measure can eliminate all risk.

8. Children’s privacy

ZELGI is for people 18 and over. If you select that you are under 18 during onboarding, the app blocks use. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 18.

9. International processing

ZELGI’s service providers may process information in the United States and other countries. If you use ZELGI from Denmark, the EU, Morocco, or elsewhere, your information may be transferred to and processed outside your country.

10. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy. When we do, we will change the “Last updated” date at the top of this page.

11. Contact
