Zelgi Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Zelgi ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores, and processes information when you use the Zelgi mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use Zelgi, we may collect the following types of information:

  • Email Address: Used to create and authenticate your account and to support account-related functionality.
  • User ID: A unique identifier associated with your Zelgi account. It is used to authenticate your account, associate your data with your account, and provide app functionality.
  • Photos: Zelgi may allow you to take or select photos for features such as profile, check-in, and image analysis. Photos may be stored securely and processed to provide these features.
  • User Content: Information you provide during onboarding and while using the app, including preferences, goals, style information, routines, scores, plans, insights, and saved looks.
  • Purchase Information: Zelgi processes subscription status and entitlement information to determine whether premium features are available to you.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use information to:

  • Create and manage your Zelgi account.
  • Authenticate users.
  • Provide Zelgi's features and functionality.
  • Process and analyze photos submitted through the app.
  • Personalize recommendations, plans, insights, and looks.
  • Determine whether your subscription or premium entitlement is active.
  • Maintain the security and functionality of the service.

3. Photos and Image Processing

If you use features that require a photo, the photo may be uploaded securely to our storage infrastructure and processed by our backend services to provide image analysis and related Zelgi functionality.

Photos are processed for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy. We do not use your photos for advertising or cross-app tracking.

4. Third-Party Services

Zelgi uses certain third-party infrastructure and services to provide the application.

Supabase

We use Supabase for authentication, account management, database storage, and secure storage of application data. Supabase may process information such as your user ID, email address, photos, and other information required to provide Zelgi's functionality.

RevenueCat

We use RevenueCat to manage in-app subscriptions and subscription entitlements. RevenueCat helps us determine whether a subscription or premium entitlement is active.

Vercel

We use Vercel to host and operate parts of our backend infrastructure. Information sent to our backend may be processed through this infrastructure in order to provide Zelgi's features.

OpenAI

Certain photos and user-provided information may be processed through our backend and sent to OpenAI services when required to provide Zelgi's AI-powered analysis and features.

5. Account Authentication

Zelgi supports account authentication using methods including email authentication and third-party sign-in providers such as Google and Apple.

Authentication information provided through these services may be processed to create and authenticate your Zelgi account.

6. Advertising and Tracking

Zelgi does not use your personal information for third-party advertising or cross-app tracking.

Zelgi does not use Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework for tracking users across apps and websites.

7. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or disclosure.

Access to user data is restricted through authentication and authorization mechanisms where appropriate.

8. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide Zelgi's services, maintain accounts, provide requested functionality, comply with legal obligations, and resolve disputes.

When information is no longer required for these purposes, it may be deleted or otherwise handled in accordance with applicable law.

9. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights relating to your personal information, including rights to access, correct, delete, or request information about how your data is processed.

If you want to request deletion of your account or personal information, please contact us using the support contact provided for Zelgi.

10. Children's Privacy

Zelgi is not intended for children under the minimum age required by applicable law. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children where such collection is prohibited by law.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make changes, we will update the "Last updated" date shown at the top of this page.

12. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how Zelgi handles personal information, please contact us through the support contact provided with the Zelgi application.
