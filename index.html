Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ZELGI (“we”, “us”, or “our”) collects, uses, stores, shares, and deletes information when you use the ZELGI iOS app.

ZELGI is an appearance, grooming, style, and progress app. It uses information you choose to provide, including photos, onboarding answers, goals, and preferences, to provide appearance analysis, personalized recommendations, routines, a plan, progress features, and optional Generate Look previews.

1. Information we collect

Depending on the features you use, ZELGI may collect or process:

  • Account information: information needed to create and authenticate your ZELGI account, such as an email address when provided by your sign-in method, and a unique account/user identifier.
  • Photos: photos you capture or select for Day 1 analysis, check-ins, progress comparisons, and Generate Look. These photos may contain your face and visible appearance.
  • Onboarding and preference information: information you choose to provide, such as age range, goals, hair and grooming information, style preferences, routine preferences, and available time.
  • Appearance analysis and app results: observations and results generated from the information you submit, such as appearance attributes, insights, opportunities, recommendations, routines, plan information, confidence information, check-in/progress information, and saved looks.
  • Generate Look information: your selected appearance directions and the generated preview image. A generated image is stored with your account when you choose to save that look.
  • Subscription information: purchase, subscription, restoration, and entitlement status needed to determine access to premium features. Apple processes App Store payments; ZELGI does not receive your full payment-card number.
  • Device-local information: limited app state, drafts, preferences, and files may be stored on your device so ZELGI can operate and preserve your progress.
  • Device and app information: limited device, platform, app-version, and device-identifier information may be processed by service providers such as RevenueCat as needed to operate subscription, entitlement, purchase-restoration, security, and service functionality.

ZELGI does not use your information for third-party advertising or cross-app advertising tracking. ZELGI does not intentionally collect your contacts, precise location, or health records as part of the features described in this policy.

2. How we use information

We use information to:

  • create, authenticate, and maintain your account;
  • perform the photo analysis you request;
  • provide personalized appearance, grooming, skin-appearance, hair, and style insights;
  • create recommendations, routines, and your plan;
  • provide check-ins and progress comparisons;
  • create Generate Look previews you explicitly request;
  • save looks when you choose to save them;
  • determine subscription and premium-feature availability;
  • operate, secure, debug, and maintain ZELGI.

3. Photos, Face Data, and AI Processing

This section specifically explains how ZELGI handles photos that may contain your face and related appearance information.

What ZELGI processes

ZELGI may process visible characteristics in a photo that are relevant to the feature you request. These can include whether a usable person/face is visible; hair length and styling; facial-hair and grooming appearance; eyebrow grooming; visible skin texture, redness, or shine; lighting; framing; pose; distance; blur; visible clothing/style; and other limited appearance-related observations. ZELGI may use those observations together with your answers and goals to create insights, opportunities, recommendations, routines, plan content, confidence information, progress comparisons, and Generate Look previews.

What ZELGI does not do

ZELGI does not use your photos to create Face ID data, perform identity verification, identify who you are by comparing your face with a database of people, or create facial-recognition identity profiles, facial embeddings, biometric templates, or unique biometric face identifiers.

This describes the current ZELGI implementation and is not intended as a legal classification of data in every jurisdiction.

OpenAI and explicit permission

ZELGI uses OpenAI as a third-party AI provider for image understanding and image generation. Before ZELGI first sends relevant photo or personal appearance information to OpenAI for these features, ZELGI presents an in-app disclosure that identifies OpenAI and asks you to select “Agree & Continue.” If you cancel instead, ZELGI does not send that information to OpenAI for the requested AI feature.

Your AI permission is separate from accepting ZELGI’s Terms or Privacy Policy, selecting a photo, or completing onboarding. ZELGI stores the permission associated with your account so it does not need to ask again for every AI request while that permission remains valid.

Feature by feature:

  • Analysis: a photo you submit and relevant feature context are sent through ZELGI’s backend to OpenAI to produce the requested appearance analysis.
  • Check-ins: a check-in photo and relevant existing appearance information may be sent through ZELGI’s backend to OpenAI to produce the requested comparison or analysis.
  • Generate Look: the source photo and the appearance/style directions you select, together with relevant ZELGI appearance information, are sent through ZELGI’s backend to OpenAI to create the requested preview.

OpenAI processes information sent to it under its applicable API terms and privacy/data policies. ZELGI does not use this policy to make a separate promise about OpenAI’s own retention periods or deletion of provider-side processing records.

ZELGI storage

Depending on the feature, photos and related information may be stored on your device, in private cloud storage associated with your ZELGI account, and in ZELGI’s database as account, profile, analysis, plan, check-in, consent, progress, quota, and saved-look records. ZELGI’s photo storage is private account storage and is not a public photo gallery.

ZELGI’s backend processes analysis and Generate Look requests in order to return results to the app. ZELGI does not intentionally use Vercel as a permanent photo library; account photos and saved content intended for continued access are stored in ZELGI’s private account storage.

4. Generate Look

Generate Look is an optional feature that creates a personalized appearance preview from a source photo and the appearance directions you select. The preview is intended as an illustrative, aspirational appearance visualization, not a guarantee of a particular real-world result.

ZELGI currently limits Generate Look generation to three successful generations per rolling seven-day period per authenticated user. ZELGI stores generation records needed to enforce this limit. Failed generation attempts are not intended to consume a successful-generation allowance. Saving an already generated look does not itself use another generation.

5. Service providers and sharing

ZELGI shares information with service providers only as needed to provide, secure, and operate the features described in this policy. We do not sell your photos or personal appearance information and do not share them for third-party advertising.

Our current service providers include:

  • Supabase: authentication, account records, database services, and private account/photo storage.
  • OpenAI: AI image understanding and Generate Look image generation, as described above.
  • Vercel: hosting for ZELGI’s backend, which processes requests between the app and supporting services.
  • RevenueCat: subscription, purchase-restoration, entitlement, and purchase/subscription-status management. RevenueCat may also process limited device and app metadata, including a device identifier such as IDFV, platform information, and app-version information, as needed to provide these services.
  • Apple: Sign in with Apple where used, App Store distribution, and App Store purchases/subscriptions.
  • Google: Google Sign-In and authentication services where used.

These providers process data under their applicable agreements and policies. ZELGI uses providers under terms and safeguards intended to protect user data consistently with the protections described in this policy and applicable platform requirements.

6. Retention

ZELGI generally keeps account-associated photos, profile information, appearance analysis, plans, check-ins, saved looks, consent records, and related account records while your ZELGI account exists and as needed to provide the service, unless you delete your account or particular content where deletion functionality is available. ZELGI currently does not promise a fixed automatic deletion period for all account content.

Some third parties, including Apple and other service providers, may retain records they are independently required or permitted to retain under their own policies or legal obligations, such as purchase records or limited processing/security records.

7. Account and data deletion

You can initiate deletion directly inside ZELGI. Go to Settings → Delete my data and follow the confirmation/reauthentication flow. You do not need to email support to initiate deletion.

When the deletion completes, ZELGI deletes the ZELGI account and associated data under ZELGI’s control, including related database records and private ZELGI photo/storage content, and clears ZELGI user data stored locally by the app. The deletion cannot be undone inside ZELGI.

If your ZELGI account uses Sign in with Apple, ZELGI’s deletion flow supports the Apple authorization/revocation process associated with deleting the account. Apple may require you to authenticate again to confirm the operation.

Deleting your ZELGI account does not by itself cancel an active App Store subscription. App Store subscriptions are managed by Apple. Provider-side records that ZELGI does not control may remain where the provider is independently required or permitted to retain them.

8. Consent and control

You can decline the AI disclosure instead of sending a photo and related appearance information to OpenAI for the requested AI feature. If you delete your ZELGI account, the ZELGI consent record associated with that account is deleted with the account.

System permissions, such as notification permission, are controlled through iOS. ZELGI treats your in-app reminder preference separately from the iOS notification authorization state.

9. Security

ZELGI uses access controls designed to restrict private account data and stored photos to the appropriate signed-in account. The app communicates with ZELGI’s backend over HTTPS. No method of storage or transmission can guarantee absolute security.

10. Children’s privacy

ZELGI is intended for people age 18 and over. The onboarding flow blocks use when a user indicates that they are under 18. ZELGI does not knowingly offer the service to children under 18.

11. International processing

ZELGI’s service providers may process information in countries other than the country where you live, including the United States. Where applicable, such processing is subject to the providers’ contractual, privacy, and legal safeguards.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as ZELGI changes. When we do, we will update the “Last updated” date above.

13. Contact

For privacy questions about ZELGI, you can use the developer contact information published for ZELGI on the App Store.
